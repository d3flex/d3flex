### Welcome to my code space                                                                                                                  
              
Hi, I'm d3flex 👋

My story with computers started early. When Linux arrived in 2000, I became interested in what was happening underneath the surface. I wanted to understand how things worked, not just use them. That curiosity led me into open source, hackerspaces and eventually a career in software engineering.

## What matters to me

- **Respect.** Life matters, and so do people's rights and dignity.
- **Building for others.** I enjoy helping when I can contribute something useful. Building something that others find useful or enjoy is rewarding in its own right.
- **Shared knowledge.** Much of what we achieve in technology comes from knowledge people have shared. Open source is the best way I know to keep that knowledge moving forward.
- **Curiosity.** Almost anything can be interesting when you look closely enough. Understanding how things work, and what they mean, is what keeps me exploring.
- **Always learning.** There is always something new to discover, to grow your mind and your view.
- **An open mind.** I try to stay open to different perspectives and ideas, including those that challenge my own.
